Read before using

GluView is not a medical device, does not diagnose, and does not replace professional guidance. Never use the displayed data as the sole basis for a treatment decision (insulin dose, food intake, etc.) — in an emergency, seek medical care immediately. See the full medical disclaimer.

What GluView is

GluView is made up of three parts:

  • Android app (also called the “collector”) — reads glucose directly from the CGM sensor over Bluetooth (Libre 2 Plus via native pipeline, Dexcom, LibreLinkUp) and can upload readings to the cloud.
  • Backend service — receives, stores and distributes readings in real time, and handles accounts/sessions, patient-caregiver links, alerts and reports. Runs under the technical name “GluSync” but is never shown under that name to end users.
  • Web portal (this same documentation domain, in production) — patient and caregiver dashboards accessible from the browser, without needing the Android app open.

Real time and history

Current glucose, trend, chart and statistics (time in range, GMI, estimated HbA1c) updated live over WebSocket, with automatic fallback if the connection drops.

Patients and caregivers

Invite someone to follow your data, or be invited yourself — with explicit control over what each person can see (current glucose, history, reports, alerts) and for how long.

Configurable alerts

Low/high glucose warnings, rapid drop or rise, and missing-data alerts — delivered through the portal feed, Pushover and browser push notifications. Always informational, never a treatment instruction.

Reports with provenance

AGP report, time in range and monthly summary, all with explicit timezone, unit, data coverage and sources — never a bare number without context on where it came from.
